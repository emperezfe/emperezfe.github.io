About the role

As a Senior Game Designer at Outfit7, I take ownership of game design with the full scope of responsibilities that seniority entails — from shaping early concepts to guiding decisions through production.

My current focus is on prototyping new mobile game ideas, working in close collaboration with product and marketing from the earliest stages. This cross-functional involvement means design decisions are grounded in both player experience and business viability from day one — not retrofitted later.

  • Concept ideation and rapid prototyping for mobile
  • Close collaboration with product and marketing on game direction
  • Multidisciplinary coordination across engineering, art and design
  • Senior design ownership: setting direction, raising the bar, unblocking teams
